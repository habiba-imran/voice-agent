Use this twelve-turn matrix, repeated to reach sample minimums:

| Case | Content | Required review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Greeting and brief explanation | No formulaic praise or repeated greetings |
| 2 | Explore a scientific question | Curiosity, at most one follow-up |
| 3 | Explain a complex mechanism | Thoughtful pacing without forced hesitation |
| 4 | Share ordinary good news | Restrained enthusiasm, unchanged facts |
| 5 | Respond to frustration | Appropriate sympathy, no false promises |
| 6 | Address uncertainty | Reassurance and explicit limits |
| 7 | Correct Tuesday to Thursday | Only warranted self-correction |
| 8 | URL, identifier, decimal, technical name | Accuracy, pronunciation, intelligibility |
| 9 | One-second hesitation and breath | No clipped user speech |
| 10 | Hmm/right/yeah during agent speech | Backchannel handling and recovery |
| 11 | Early/late “Wait, stop, I meant...” | Yield, no obsolete tail or duplicate reply |
| 12 | Consecutive follow-ups, then silence | Context, varied openings, restrained fillers |

Include noise, abandoned speech and cancellation. Evaluate initial AEC behavior separately from
warmed turns. Never change factual answers solely to improve prosody.

Log date/time/timezone, machine/region/network, SDK/model versions, authorized voice, prompt hash,
non-secret configuration, cold/warm status, counts/errors and observable usage/cost/cache tokens.
Use synthetic content. Persist no raw audio/transcript without consent. Human listeners must
hear actual synthesized speech; text, fake PCM and model probabilities cannot score speech quality.

Score 1–5 for naturalness, pitch/intonation, emotion fit, rhythm/pauses, filler placement/sparsity,
pronunciation/intelligibility, continuity/artifacts and interruption experience. Add evidence notes
and an A/B preference or tie. Count filler-bearing replies, repeated openings, factual errors and
clipped turns; inspect meaningful words such as “actually” separately from nonlexical hesitations.

Acceptance: average listening score at least 4/5, no severe quality/accuracy failures and no
interruption regression against baseline. Report E2E, EOU and audio-to-stop p50/p95 with sample
counts and real playback evidence. Keep humanization off if benefit is unclear. Additional
markup, breaks, laughter and voice changes need their own tested proposal.
